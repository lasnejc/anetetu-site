In English

Coach Forme is a French-language AI training coach for runners, trail runners, triathletes and people who combine endurance sport with strength training. Each morning it reads the athlete's sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and recent training load, sets a daily readiness level, chooses the right session and sends it as a structured workout to the athlete's watch calendar. In the evening the athlete reports perceived effort and pain, and the coach adjusts the following days. Athletes can also chat with the coach about goals, schedule changes and fatigue.

Device data is accessed only through official manufacturer programmes, with the athlete's explicit consent, and is used solely to deliver the coaching service. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. Data is hosted in the European Union and can be deleted at any time.
