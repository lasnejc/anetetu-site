1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is AVEO SOLUTIONS, publisher of Coach Forme.

  • French limited liability company (SARL) registered with the Marseille Trade and Companies Register under number 892 691 296
  • Registered office: 21 rue Haxo, 13001 Marseille, France

AVEO SOLUTIONS is not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer. The Coach Forme team handles all data requests directly.

2. Summary

  • We use your data only to coach you: compute your daily readiness, build and adapt your plan, send you your workouts and answer your questions.
  • We never sell your data, we show no advertising, and we do not use your data to train AI models.
  • Your data is hosted in the European Union (Microsoft Azure).
  • Your watch connects through the manufacturer's official access. We never see your password.
  • You can disconnect your watch, export or delete your data at any time.

4. Data from your watch (Garmin and other manufacturers)

Connection

You connect your watch from Coach Forme. You are redirected to the manufacturer's sign-in page (for example Garmin Connect), where you choose which data to share. We receive an access token, never your password.

Garmin data we receive

  • Activities: sport type, date, duration, distance, pace or speed, heart rate, power, cadence, laps, elevation, GPS track and activity file (.FIT).
  • Daily health summaries: sleep and sleep stages, HRV, resting heart rate, stress, energy level (Body Battery), steps and calories.

We do not request access to women's health data (cycle, pregnancy).

Data we send to Garmin

With your consent, we send your structured workouts and plan to your Garmin Connect calendar so that they appear on your watch.

Our commitments for this data

  • It is used solely to provide the coaching service. It is never sold, rented, used for advertising or used to train AI models.
  • It is not shared with any third party, except the technical processors listed in section 7, which act only on our instructions.
  • It is never transferred to another training platform or service.
  • In the app, data from your watch is attributed to its source.

Disconnection

You can disconnect your watch at any time, from Coach Forme or from the manufacturer's app (in Garmin Connect: connected apps section). We then immediately stop receiving your data, and the data received from that manufacturer is deleted within 30 days.

Garmin, COROS and other manufacturers are responsible for the data kept in their own services, which is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. How we use artificial intelligence

  • You are interacting with an AI. Coach messages are generated automatically by a large language model (Azure OpenAI, hosted in the European Union).
  • Clear rules. Your daily readiness is set by explicit rules (for example: HRV below your normal range and low recovery, so the session is made easier). The AI explains these rules and writes the workout.
  • Minimal data. We only send the model what it needs to answer: your first name, athlete profile, recent data and your message. Never your email address or phone number.
  • No model training. Neither Coach Forme nor Microsoft uses your data to train AI models. Microsoft may retain requests for up to 30 days solely for abuse monitoring.
  • You stay in control. The coach's proposals are training suggestions. You can decline them, change them or ask for an explanation. No decision producing legal effects or similarly significantly affecting you is made automatically.
  • Not medical advice. The AI can make mistakes. Coach Forme is not a medical device. If you have sharp, persistent or unusual pain, consult a healthcare professional.

10. Security

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Secrets and access tokens kept in an encrypted vault (Azure Key Vault). Resources accessed with managed identities, with no passwords stored in code.
  • Access restricted to the Coach Forme team, with multi-factor authentication.
  • No watch password is ever requested or stored.
  • In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk, we notify the French data protection authority (CNIL) within 72 hours and, where the risk is high, the people concerned.

11. Your rights

You have the following rights over your data: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability (export of your data in a machine-readable format), objection, and withdrawal of consent at any time. Under French law, you can also give instructions on what happens to your data after your death.

To exercise your rights, email privacy@anetetu.fr. We reply within one month. You can also delete your account directly by writing "delete my account" to the coach.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL: www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes · 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France.

12. Minimum age

Coach Forme is for people aged 18 and over.

13. Cookies

14. Changes to this policy
